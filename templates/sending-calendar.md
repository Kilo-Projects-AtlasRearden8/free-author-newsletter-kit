## Suggested cadence (pick one and adjust)

| Frequency | Emails per quarter | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | 3 | Testing the habit, low-energy weeks |
| Every two weeks | 6 | Most authors and newsletter beginners |
| Weekly | 12 | Launches, promotions, high-engagement lists |

Start smaller than you think. You can always add sends later.

## Topic ideas to slot in

- Welcome series reminder (resend to new subscribers)
- New book or guide release announcement
- Launch-week last-call email
- Behind-the-scenes writing update
- Tool or prompt recommendation (AI, writing, publishing)
- Reader question Q&A
- Sale or discount reminder
- "Best of" roundup at quarter end
