## Incentives that cost nothing

- Mention the newsletter in every book's back matter
- Pin the signup link to your social profiles
- Add a QR code to bookmarks or business cards
- Offer early access to cover reveals or release dates
- Reply personally to the first few subscribers (builds loyalty and
  deliverability)
