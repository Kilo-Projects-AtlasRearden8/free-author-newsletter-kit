# Welcome Email Template

Fill in the bracketed fields, then paste into your email tool (MailerLite,
ConvertKit, Substack, etc.). Or open `welcome-email.html` in your browser,
fill it in there, and print to paper or PDF.

author newsletter. Sets expectations, delivers the promised freebie, and
invites a reply so your future emails land in the primary inbox.
